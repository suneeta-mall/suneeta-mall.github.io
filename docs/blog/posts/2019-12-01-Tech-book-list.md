# Reading List

A curated collection of books that have shaped my thinking across technology, leadership, creativity, and life. These are books I've genuinely enjoyed and found valuable, organized by theme.

---

## Artificial Intelligence & The Future

- **[Life 3.0: Being Human in the Age of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.amazon.com.au/Life-3-0-Being-Artificial-Intelligence/dp/1101946598)** by Max Tegmark
  Explores how AI is transforming civilization and what it means to be human in an age of intelligent machines.

- **[A Brief History of Intelligence](https://www.amazon.com.au/Brief-History-Intelligence-Humans-Breakthroughs/dp/0063286343)** by Max S. Bennett
  Traces the evolutionary development of human cognition and connects biological intelligence principles to modern AI.

- **[The Quest for Artificial Intelligence](https://ai.stanford.edu/~nilsson/QAI/qai.pdf)** by Nils J. Nilsson
  A comprehensive history of AI from one of the field's pioneers.

---

## Machine Learning & Deep Learning

- **[Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective](https://www.amazon.com.au/Machine-Learning-Probabilistic-Perspective-Computation/dp/0262018020)** by Kevin P. Murphy
  The definitive probabilistic approach to machine learning.

- **[Neural Networks and Deep Learning](http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/)** by Michael Nielsen
  An accessible online book that builds intuition for neural networks.

- **[Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)** by Ian Goodfellow et al.
  The comprehensive textbook on deep learning fundamentals.

- **[Machine Learning Yearning](https://www.deeplearning.ai/programs/machine-learning-yearning/)** by Andrew Ng
  Practical advice on structuring ML projects from one of the field's best teachers.

---

## Statistics & Mathematics

- **[Linear Algebra Done Right](https://linear.axler.net/)** by Sheldon Axler
  A conceptually clear approach to linear algebra.

- **[Mathematical Statistics and Data Analysis](https://www.amazon.com/Mathematical-Statistics-Data-Analysis-John/dp/0534209343)** by John A. Rice
  Rigorous foundations in statistical methods.

- **[Elements of Statistical Learning](https://web.stanford.edu/~hastie/Papers/ESLII.pdf)** by Trevor Hastie et al.
  The advanced reference for statistical learning methods.

- **[Introduction to Statistical Learning](https://www.statlearning.com/)** by Trevor Hastie et al.
  A more accessible companion to Elements, with R examples.

---

## Software Engineering & Systems

- **[Code: The Hidden Language of Computer Hardware and Software](https://www.amazon.com.au/Code-Language-Computer-Developer-Practices-ebook/dp/B00JDMPOK2)** by Charles Petzold
  A masterful journey from telegraph to computer, building understanding from first principles.

- **[The Pragmatic Programmer](https://www.amazon.com.au/Pragmatic-Programmer-journey-mastery-Anniversary-ebook/dp/B07VRS84D1)** by Andrew Hunt & David Thomas
  Timeless principles for software craftsmanship.

- **[Coders at Work](https://www.amazon.com.au/Coders-Work-Reflections-Craft-Programming/dp/1430219483)** by Peter Seibel
  Fascinating interviews with legendary programmers.

- **[Clean Code](https://www.amazon.com.au/Clean-Code-Handbook-Software-Craftsmanship/dp/0132350882)** by Robert C. Martin
  Principles for writing readable, maintainable code.

- **[Real-World Bug Hunting](https://www.amazon.com/Real-World-Bug-Hunting-Field-Hacking/dp/1593278616)** by Peter Yaworski
  Practical field guide to finding security vulnerabilities.

- **[The Book of Why](https://www.amazon.com.au/Book-Why-Science-Cause-Effect/dp/046509760X/)** by Judea Pearl
  A paradigm shift in thinking about causality versus correlation.

---

## Kubernetes & Cloud Native

- **[Kubernetes: Up and Running](https://www.amazon.com.au/Kubernetes-Up-Running-Brendan-Burns/dp/1492046531/)** by Kelsey Hightower, Joe Beda & Brendan Burns
  The essential introduction from Kubernetes creators.

- **[Kubernetes Security](https://learning.oreilly.com/library/view/kubernetes-security/9781492039075/)** by Michael Hausenblas & Liz Rice
  Security-first approach to Kubernetes deployments.

- **[Container Security](https://learning.oreilly.com/library/view/container-security/9781492056690/)** by Liz Rice
  Deep dive into container security fundamentals.

- **[Kubernetes for Developers](https://learning.oreilly.com/library/view/kubernetes-for-developers/9781788834759/)** by Joseph Heck
  Developer-focused perspective on Kubernetes.

---

## Leadership & Communication

- **[Leadership on the Line](https://www.amazon.com.au/Leadership-Line-Staying-Through-Dangers/dp/1578514371)** by Ronald A. Heifetz & Marty Linsky
  How to navigate the dangers and challenges of leadership while staying effective and maintaining integrity.

- **[Leadership Is Language](https://www.amazon.com.au/Leadership-Language-Changes-Difference-Results-ebook/dp/B07KMQ6CVW)** by L. David Marquet
  How what you say (and don't say) shapes leadership effectiveness and organizational outcomes.

- **[The Next Conversation](https://www.amazon.com.au/Next-Conversation-Argue-Less-Talk-ebook/dp/B0DHK9TV3J)** by Jefferson Fisher
  How to argue less and talk more—practical approaches to productive dialogue.

- **[Wise Guy: Lessons from a Life](https://www.amazon.com.au/Wise-Guy-Lessons-Life-Kawasaki/dp/0525538615)** by Guy Kawasaki
  Life lessons and wisdom from Silicon Valley's legendary evangelist.

---

## Thinking & Decision Making

- **[Thinking, Fast and Slow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking,_Fast_and_Slow)** by Daniel Kahneman
  Nobel laureate's exploration of the two systems that drive how we think.

- **[Mindset: The New Psychology of Success](https://www.amazon.com.au/Mindset-Carol-S-Dweck/dp/0345472322)** by Carol S. Dweck
  The groundbreaking research on fixed vs. growth mindsets.

- **[Drive](https://www.amazon.com.au/Drive-Surprising-Truth-About-Motivates/dp/1594484805)** by Daniel H. Pink
  The science of motivation beyond carrots and sticks.

- **[Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less](https://www.amazon.com.au/Essentialism-Disciplined-Pursuit-Greg-McKeown/dp/0804137382)** by Greg McKeown
  The philosophy of doing less but better.

---

## Innovation & Entrepreneurship

- **[The Innovator's Dilemma](https://www.amazon.com/The-Innovators-Dilemma-Technologies-Cause/dp/0875845851)** by Clayton M. Christensen
  Why great companies fail when disruptive technologies emerge.

- **[Zero to One](https://www.amazon.com.au/Zero-One-Notes-Startups-Future/dp/0804139296)** by Peter Thiel & Blake Masters
  Contrarian thinking about creating new value.

---

## Writing & Creativity

- **[If You Want to Write](https://www.amazon.com.au/You-Want-Write-Brenda-Ueland/dp/9650060286)** by Brenda Ueland
  A timeless classic on creativity, artistic independence, and finding your voice.

---

## Education

- **[Creating Schools Where Students and Teachers Want to Be](https://melbournebooks.com.au/products/creating-schools-where-students-and-teachers-want-to-be)** by Michael Lawrence & Fabio D'Agostin
  Rethinking education through community collaboration and meaningful reform.

---

## Parenting

- **[The Explosive Child](https://www.amazon.com.au/Explosive-Child-Sixth-Understanding-Chronically/dp/0063092468)** by Ross W. Greene
  A collaborative problem-solving approach for understanding and parenting easily frustrated, chronically inflexible children.

- **[Becoming Brilliant](https://www.amazon.com.au/Becoming-Brilliant-Science-Successful-Children/dp/1433822393/)** by Roberta Golinkoff & Kathryn Hirsh-Pasek
  Research-backed approach to nurturing children's development.

- **[Thinking Parent, Thinking Child](https://www.amazon.com/Thinking-Parent-Child-Everyday-Solutions/dp/0878227032)** by Myrna B. Shure
  Practical problem-solving approaches for parents.

- **[The Psychology of Babies](https://www.amazon.com.au/Psychology-Babies-relationships-support-development-ebook/dp/B0070TRFIO)** by Lynne Murray
  Understanding early development through the lens of relationships.

---

## Science & Nature

- **[The End of Ice](https://www.amazon.com/End-Ice-Bearing-Witness-Disruption-ebook/dp/B079G4NJVD)** by Dahr Jamail
  Bearing witness to climate disruption and finding meaning in the crisis.

- **[The Second Kind of Impossible](https://www.amazon.com.au/Second-Kind-Impossible-Extraordinary-Matter/dp/1476729921/)** by Paul Steinhardt
  The extraordinary quest to discover quasicrystals—a new form of matter.
