# To all the Daminis

## The Poem

<div style="text-align: center; margin: 2em 0; font-family: 'Georgia', serif;">

<div style="margin: 1.5em 0; line-height: 1.8;">
Can't believe you had to go through this<br>
Can't believe someone did this to you
</div>

<div style="margin: 1.5em 0; line-height: 1.8;">
Took a part of you away<br>
Shattering your soul into pieces
</div>

<div style="margin: 1.5em 0; line-height: 1.8;">
As my heart melts away<br>
I have very less to say<br>
Not that I am callous<br>
But hey! no false promises
</div>

<div style="margin: 1.5em 0; line-height: 1.8;">
As I read about you<br>
I feel proud of you<br>
On your courage<br>
And your strength
</div>

<div style="margin: 1.5em 0; line-height: 1.8;">
It's not just you Damini<br>
Atrocities are such<br>
we are a land of Daminis<br>
Some faded, some long forgotten
</div>

<div style="margin: 1.5em 0; line-height: 1.8;">
I ain't know, who to blame<br>
So I blame myself<br>
For a being a part of community<br>
Who could not keep you safe
</div>

<div style="margin: 1.5em 0; line-height: 1.8;">
I ain't no feminist, but I do have soul<br>
For I am female<br>
And I know how you feel<br>
I know how you feel
</div>

<div style="margin: 1.5em 0; line-height: 1.8;">
As millions of hearts reach out to you<br>
To share your pain & your agony<br>
All I can offer you is pray<br>
Love & Support
</div>

</div>

## Inspiration

An atrocious incident in [Dec of 2012, New Delhi][delhi_damini_case]

[delhi_damini_case]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Delhi_gang_rape
