# Poetry: Where Logic Meets Language

As an ML engineer, I think in systems, patterns, and probabilities. As a poet, I translate those technical experiences into verse—exploring the intersection of algorithms and human experience, the humor in debugging sessions at 2am, and the deeper questions about where technology is taking us.

These poems emerged from the same curiosity that drives my technical work: a desire to understand, question, and make sense of the world around us.

## Recently Published

### [Singularity](./singularity.md)
*Sept 2024* | :material-music: Audio Available

A meditation on self-belief, inner strength, and finding your unique power in an age of convergence. Written during a period of professional transition, this poem explores the tension between doubt and conviction, urging readers to embrace their authentic selves.

> *"Feel it! Believe it! Find it all, within!"*

**Themes:** Personal empowerment, authenticity, transformation

---

### [Life of AI Engineers](./life-of-ai-engineer.md)
*June 2024* | :material-music: Audio Available

A humorous, rhythmic tribute to the peculiar existence of ML practitioners—where exploding gradients, OOM errors, and CUDA crashes are daily companions. Equal parts comedy and confession, this poem captures the rollercoaster reality of training models and debugging neural networks.

> *"Floating points all day long / My AI kryptonite is python song"*

**Themes:** AI engineering, technical humor, perseverance

## About This Collection

Poetry offers something that technical documentation cannot: space for ambiguity, emotion, and the messiness of human experience. These works don't aim to explain or solve—they explore, question, and occasionally celebrate the absurdities of working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and very real human challenges.

If you're interested in how technical thinking influences creative writing (or vice versa), you might enjoy my blog posts on [Cassie 2.0](../blog/posts/2025-05-10-cassie-2.0.md) and [ChatGPT vs Me as a Children's Book Author](../blog/posts/2024-01-07-ChatGPT_vs_me_kids_book_author.md).

---

*These creative works reflect a parallel journey to my technical publications—both driven by curiosity, iteration, and a desire to make sense of complex systems, whether they're neural networks or human experiences.*
